Reach the right HR, faster.

HireReach finds the recruiters and HR people among your LinkedIn connections, writes each one a personal message with your resume attached, and sends them one by one while you stay in control.

Four steps, no copy and paste

You set it up once. HireReach does the repetitive clicking, and you decide who gets a message.

STEP 1

Filter

Choose the job titles you care about, like HR, Recruiter or Talent Acquisition, then scan your connections.

STEP 2

Matches

Review who matched. Tick or untick people before anything is sent.

STEP 3

Message

Write one message. Each person's first name is filled in for you. Attach your resume if you like.

STEP 4

Send

Press Send & Next. HireReach opens each chat, fills in the message, attaches the file and sends it.

Built to be careful

Automation on a professional network should be polite and predictable.

Skips people you already messagedIf you wrote to someone in the last 2 days, or the chat already has a message from today or yesterday, they are skipped.
You can stop any timeSkip a person or stop the whole run with one click.
Daily limit reminderA gentle warning to keep it to about 20 to 30 messages a day.
Stays on your computerNo server, no sign-up, no data collected. Everything runs inside your own browser.

What it is made of

The same stack in plain words, so anyone can follow along.

PartWhat it is
Chrome extensionA small add-on installed inside the Chrome browser, like an ad blocker.
JavaScriptThe language that tells the extension what to click and type.
CSS and SVGThe design. CSS styles the panel and SVG draws the logo and icons sharply.
Shadow DOMA protective bubble so the panel looks the same on every page.
Browser storageA small notebook that remembers your keywords, your message and who you already messaged.
File APILets you attach your resume and send it with each message.
Git and GitHubSaves every version of the code and lets anyone read it.
No serverNo databaseNo loginFree

Try it yourself

  1. Download the project from GitHub and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the hirereach folder.
  4. Open your LinkedIn connections page, refresh, and click HireReach.
